Frequently asked questions

How do I restore Remove Ads?

Once purchase restoration is available in the released game, use the Restore Purchases option in the Shop while signed into the Apple Account that made the purchase. If you need help, contact support.

Why is a purchase unavailable?

Check your internet connection and try restarting the app. Products may also be unavailable in some regions or during store interruptions.

How do I report a bug?

Email support with steps to reproduce the problem and, if possible, a screenshot.
